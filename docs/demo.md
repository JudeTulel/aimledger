# Demo walkthrough

1. Open the companion trainer at https://judetulel.github.io/aimledger/
2. Start a run and hit as many targets as you can in 20 seconds.
3. Copy the score from the result panel.
4. Open the realm at https://gno.land/r/g16aehkv63k62ege7z7c7g5zc58mljklvtdkxd3u/aimledger
5. Join (free), then paste the score into the SubmitScore form and sign with a Gno.land wallet on testnet.
6. Watch the leaderboard and your credit balance update. Repeat on the next UTC day to grow a streak.
7. Once you have at least 100 credits, redeem them with the Claim form to receive testnet GNOT from the prize pool.
